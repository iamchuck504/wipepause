Your privacy

Your typing stays yours.

Privacy policy · Updated September 9, 2026
Developer: Carlos Lopez

Typing and pointer activity

WipePause does not collect, record, store, or transmit typed text or pointer activity. During cleaning, it discards ordinary input events delivered to its own windows. Escape timing and press counts are used temporarily in memory for the exit gesture.

What stays on your Mac

Your preferences, such as duration, appearance, and exit gesture, are stored locally. A temporary recovery record contains previous and applied keyboard mappings and device-service identifiers, not typed text. It is removed after verified restoration. If recovery is interrupted, the app retries automatically after a helper failure and when the app opens. A recovery alert also offers a retry without starting a cleaning session.

Permissions and network access

The current version does not request Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, or app network access. Opening a support or privacy link uses your browser or email app.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request. Please do not include passwords or sensitive content. You can ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to any required retention.

This website

This site does not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. The website hosting provider and email provider may process technical information under their own privacy policies to deliver these services. This website is hosted by Vercel. A version is also available through GitHub Pages.
