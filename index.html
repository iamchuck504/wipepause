Scrubook

Scrubook pauses your Mac's keyboard and trackpad so you can wipe them clean — nothing gets typed, nothing gets clicked. Available on the Mac App Store.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: open an issue and you'll get a reply there.

Getting out of Cleaning Mode

Press Esc five times quickly, or hold Esc for five seconds. Input also comes back on its own when the timer ends, when the Mac sleeps or the screen locks — and immediately if the app quits for any reason. Force Quit (⌘⌥Esc) and the power button are never blocked.

Privacy

Scrubook records nothing and sends nothing. It asks for one permission, Accessibility, on first launch, and uses it only to block keys during a cleaning session. Read the privacy policy.
